About FinNews AI

FinNews AI is an artificial-intelligence powered financial news platform built to solve a universal problem: financial jargon overload.

Every day, major financial publications publish articles filled with complex acronyms (CPI, FOMC, WTI, H100), dense accounting terms (gross margins, basis points, yields), and obscure central bank language. FinNews AI acts as an instant translator, distilling complex financial events into clear takeaways, key numbers, and beginner-friendly vocabulary.
